About Dropeo

Dropeo is a small online shop selling practical, good-looking products for the home and kitchen – things like stone dish drying mats, reusable bread bags, laundry helpers and self-care tools. Our shop runs on Etsy, where you can see every product, read reviews and order safely.

Dropeo on Pinterest

We share our own products on our own Pinterest account so people can save ideas and find our shop. To keep this consistent, we use a small in-house tool that:

  • reads the public details of our own Etsy listings (title, description and photos),
  • creates Pins on our own Pinterest business account, each linking back to the product on Etsy.

The tool is used only by Dropeo for Dropeo's own account. It does not post for other people and does not collect data about Pinterest users. Read our Privacy Policy.
