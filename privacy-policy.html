Dropeo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dropeo ("we", "us") handles information on this website and in the in-house tool we use to publish our own products to Pinterest (the "Dropeo Pin Tool"). Dropeo is an online shop operating on Etsy at etsy.com/shop/Dropeo.

1. Who uses the Dropeo Pin Tool

The Dropeo Pin Tool is a private tool used only by Dropeo to publish Pins to Dropeo's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and does not let other people sign in or connect their accounts.

2. What information the tool uses

  • Our own Etsy listing details – product titles, descriptions, tags and photos of Dropeo's own listings, to create Pins about those products.
  • Our own Pinterest account access – an access token for Dropeo's own Pinterest account, used only to read our own boards (to choose which board to post to) and to create Pins on our own account.

The tool does not collect, store or process personal information about Pinterest users, Etsy buyers or website visitors. It does not read other people's Pins, boards, followers, messages or analytics.

3. How the information is used

The information above is used only to create and publish Pins that show Dropeo products and link to their Etsy listings. We keep a simple record of which of our listings have already been pinned (listing numbers, Pin numbers and dates) so the same Pin is not published twice.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data obtained through the Pinterest API with anyone. Data is sent only to Pinterest itself, to publish our Pins.

5. Storage and security

Access keys and tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published. Only Dropeo has access to them. You can learn how Pinterest handles data in the Pinterest Privacy Policy.

6. This website

This website is a simple information page. It has no sign-up, no forms and no advertising trackers, and it does not ask you for personal information. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Orders

Orders are placed and paid on Etsy. Information you give when buying is handled under the Etsy Privacy Policy and used by Dropeo only to fulfil your order.

8. Data deletion

We can remove any Pin we published, and delete our posting records, on request. Revoking the tool's access to our Pinterest account removes its ability to post.

9. Changes

If we change how the tool works, we will update this page and the date at the top.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, please message us through our Etsy shop: etsy.com/shop/Dropeo.
